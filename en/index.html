Projects and Case Studies

Real-world experience presented without exposing corporate data

ITSM — Centralized Service and Asset Management

Anonymized real-world scenario: an environment with separate tools for service desk, inventory and remote support, resulting in vendor dependency and limited operational standardization.

Role: implementation and continuous improvement of GLPI in containers, LDAP integration, configuration of entities, profiles, groups, service catalog, SLA/OLA and reports; adoption of an internally managed remote access solution.

Result: consolidation of ITSM and inventory functions, greater internal control of the environment and reduced dependency on previously used third-party tool contracts.

Automation — WhatsApp Service Integrated with Business Processes

Anonymized real-world scenario: requests and field information received through messages, creating risks of data loss, rework and limited traceability.

Role: design of a conversational workflow integrated through n8n and a WhatsApp API, PostgreSQL and Redis, handling text, audio, photos, videos and documents, as well as notifications and status updates.

Result: a functional MVP capable of registering requests, organizing attachments, routing responsible teams and maintaining a structured process history.

Data & BI — Sales Management Flash Dashboard

Anonymized real-world scenario: the need to transform dispersed commercial data into a recurring and objective management view.

Role: preparation and integration of data sources, definition of rules and KPIs, analytical modeling and development of Power BI views for performance monitoring.

Result: consolidated and standardized information, reducing manual preparation effort and improving analysis of results and variances.

Data Integration — ERP Extraction for an Analytical Layer

Anonymized real-world scenario: the need to make corporate ERP data available for external consumption without affecting transactional operations.

Role: proof of concept for controlled data extraction, generation of structured files and design of the analytical consumption process.

Result: technical validation of a simple and traceable alternative for feeding analytics and integrations without exposing production environment details.

SAP — Support and Ticket Governance

Anonymized real-world scenario: distributed operations across multiple business units, functional queues and external consulting support, requiring close monitoring after significant environment changes.

Role: ticket monitoring, triage by business unit and queue, identification of responsible teams, analysis of ticket aging and last interaction, plus structured follow-up with consulting partners and requesters.

Result: greater visibility into pending items, ownership and ticket aging, supporting prioritization and reducing tickets without updates.

Access Control — Automation and Operational Analysis

Anonymized real-world scenario: the need to transform operational access records into useful information for control, auditing and analysis.

Role: development of data automation and integration solutions using Microsoft ecosystem tools, including Power Automate, OneDrive and Power BI.

Result: a recurring and structured process with less manual intervention and consolidated information for monitoring.
